Shrey · Websites

I build websites the way I fly. Checklist first, then the fun part.

Hi, I'm Shrey. I trained as a commercial pilot, and somewhere between the logbooks and the layovers I started building websites. It turned out I liked it more than I expected, mostly because it's the same kind of thinking: plan carefully, check everything, and make the complicated bits feel smooth for whoever's on board.

I design and develop on Wix, Wix Studio, WordPress and custom HTML, CSS and JavaScript, including the backend work most people would rather not think about.

I work from wherever Kenny and I are living that month. You get regular updates, clear timelines and someone who actually answers messages. Time zones have never been a problem. Airport Wi-Fi occasionally has.

How I work

Four stages, no turbulence

You'll always know which stage we're in and what I need from you next.

  1. 1 Discover A call to understand your business, your customers and what the site actually needs to do.
  2. 2 Structure Pages, content and the right platform, mapped out before anything gets designed.
  3. 3 Design The look and feel, shared early so you can give feedback while it's still easy to change.
  4. 4 Build & launch Development, testing on real devices, launch, and a short handover so you're comfortable running it.
