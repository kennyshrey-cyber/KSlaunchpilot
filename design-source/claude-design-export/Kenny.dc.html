Marketing that sounds like you on a good day.

Hi, I'm Kenny. I'm a digital marketer and a yoga teacher, and I've stopped pretending those are two separate parts of my life. Both are about paying attention to people and being clear about what you're offering.

I work with small businesses, mostly in hospitality and wellness, on brand, strategy and content. I help you work out what makes you worth choosing, then show it in a way that feels honest and doesn't need you posting five times a day.

I work remotely while Shrey and I travel. Mornings are for deep work, afternoons for calls, and I've learned to plan content around ferry schedules.

How I work

Slow at the start, so it's easy later

Most of the work happens before anything gets posted. That's on purpose.

  1. 1 Listen A long conversation about your business, your guests or clients, and what's working now.
  2. 2 Position We agree on who you're for and how you want to be described. Everything else builds on this.
  3. 3 Plan Channels, content pillars and a calendar that matches your actual capacity.
  4. 4 Create & refine We make the content, see what people respond to, and adjust month by month.

On the mat

What yoga taught me about marketing

Teaching yoga means watching how people actually respond, not how you hoped they would. I bring the same habit to marketing. Less noise, more attention.

So I work calmly and on purpose. No panic posting, no trends you'll regret, no pretending to be a bigger brand than you are. Just steady, human marketing that fits the way you run your business.
