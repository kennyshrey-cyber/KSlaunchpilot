The studio

A two-person studio, on purpose.

We're Kenny and Shrey. KS Launch Pilot brings marketing strategy together with design, development and technical execution, so the strategic and technical sides of a digital project sit under one roof.

We stay small deliberately. Clients work directly with the two people doing the work, without account managers or hand-offs in between. That keeps decisions quick and the thinking consistent from first call to launch.

We take on a limited number of projects at a time, and we prefer long-term relationships with businesses that care about quality over one-off jobs.
