About us

How we ended up here

We met in India. It was not a movie moment. It was a normal one that turned out to matter a lot.

Then came long distance: different countries, different time zones, a lot of voice notes and a lot of "can you hear me now?". We figured out pretty quickly that we wanted the same thing, which was a life that didn't need an office or a fixed address.

We reunited in Thailand, quit our old routines, and started working online full time. Since then we've lived in Vietnam, Bali, Lombok, Kuala Lumpur, the Philippines and back in India, usually a few months at a time.

Balance is the wellness side and the partnership. Baggage is the travel, the stories and everything we carry along the way. Both are equally true.
