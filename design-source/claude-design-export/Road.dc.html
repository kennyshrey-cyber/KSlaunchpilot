Destinations

Places we've called home

01

Thailand

Where it all became real. We learned how to share a small desk, how to split the cooking, and that Shrey will eat the same khao soi every day if nobody stops him.

Cafe
A garden cafe in Nimman with strong coffee and slow afternoons
Stay
Our first shared apartment, fourth floor, no lift
Moment
The Sunday walking street, getting lost on purpose
02

Vietnam

The best coffee we've had anywhere, and the country where working remotely finally felt normal. Kenny found her morning routine here. Shrey found egg coffee.

Cafe
A tiny coffee shop in Da Nang with plastic stools and perfect ca phe sua da
Stay
A flat near My Khe beach with a balcony we worked from every morning
Moment
Riding the Hai Van Pass and stopping far too often for photos
03

Bali

Busy, beautiful and full of people doing exactly what we do. Kenny taught more classes here than anywhere else. We also got very good at avoiding traffic.

Cafe
A rice-field cafe in Ubud that opens early enough for the first call of the day
Stay
A small villa with an outdoor shower and a resident gecko
Moment
Sunrise at Mount Batur, which Shrey complained about until the sun came up
04

Kuala Lumpur

After the islands, a city felt good. Reliable internet, big deadlines handled calmly, and some of the best food of the whole trip, mostly eaten at hawker stalls.

Cafe
A quiet specialty coffee bar in Bangsar with proper desks
Stay
A high-rise apartment with a pool we used exactly twice
Moment
Late-night roti canai after launching a client site
05

Lombok

Slower than anywhere we've been. Empty beaches, patchy signal and the kind of quiet that makes you do your best work. We still talk about going back.

Cafe
A surf cafe in Kuta Lombok where the Wi-Fi works if you sit by the window
Stay
A bungalow on a hill with a view worth the scooter ride up
Moment
A power cut, a candle and the most honest conversation of the year
Life on the road

The real side of it

It's a good life. It's also laundry, visa runs and hunting for a power socket. Here's what a normal week looks like.

Working from cafes

We test the Wi-Fi before we order. Calls happen at home. Deep work happens wherever the coffee is good and the chairs aren't terrible.

Routines

Yoga first, work second, exploring after. Keeping the same shape to our days is what makes moving every few months manageable.

Travel days

Shrey plans them like a flight: route, backups, snacks. We block the calendar and never schedule client calls on a moving day.

The less glamorous bits

Missing family. Bad mattresses. Rainy season. Being together all the time and learning when the other one needs an afternoon alone.
